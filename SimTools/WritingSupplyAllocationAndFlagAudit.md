## Allocation: a narrower, observable opportunity

The stock diagnosis reproduces exactly: 3,202 of 4,693 newly recorded demos have
a released professional album use and no observed professional single use.
All 3,202 also join a released album's slate master prepared in the same week
as the demo's first `Recorded` snapshot. These are actual album preparations,
not just professionally authored songs appearing on some later album.

The new analyzer joins song/first client, label, album master and dated single
intentions. For each demo it takes the last observed single-plan state at or
before first recording, requires an open plan intended within the following
26 weeks, and joins the earliest subsequently issued qualifying plan through its
actual record ID. Only demos first recorded by week 78 receive the complete
26-week follow-up. Opening-recorded stock and any professional single use are
excluded. Multiple album appearances count once.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Album only at endpoint | 1,097 | 1,116 | 989 | 3,202 |
| Recorded by week 78 | 875 | 890 | 808 | 2,573 |

| Next issued single still unprepared at recording snapshot | 199 | 183 | 189 | 571 |
| Unprepared single and album hook at least .10 higher | 71 | 63 | 78 | 212 |

The 866 observations refer to **527 distinct single releases**, because several
album songs can face the same next single. The 571 unprepared-plan observations
refer to 360 singles. The 212 larger-hook opportunities refer to **165 distinct
singles** (52/50/63 per world); they are not 212 possible extra releases or
observed substitutions. The .10 gap is a diagnostic cut, not a calibrated
selection threshold. Roughly half of the 866 album songs have a lower hook than
the subsequent single: 68% album-only is not itself evidence of universal waste.

For example, in seed 1001, `song_pub_detroit_00174` was recorded for
`artist_00934` / `label_0040` in week 48 and appears on `project_1934`.
Its composition hook is .962 and its recorded license fee is $145. An existing,
then-unprepared single plan issued `gen_6550` in week 53 using artist-written
material with composition hook .310. This identifies a reviewable opportunity;
it does not establish that the album performance was a better commercial single.
The generated JSON retains twenty large-gap examples per seed.

### What the code actually permits

1. `ApplyIdentityToAlbumTrack` marks the professional demo recorded when the LP
   track is made. `ProfessionalWritingMarket.Visible` then excludes it from
   ordinary professional discovery. This is the correct first-recording boundary.
2. `CanLicense` continues to recognize the original label/act's control, and
   `LicenseFee` returns zero for its recorded demo. A new license is not inherently
   required to use the client's own song. Master ownership is a separate question.
3. `CreatePromoSingleFromAlbum` already retains the chosen album performance's
   song and master under the default-on Polar path. Neither Polar selection nor
   master-preserving promotion is accidentally off.
4. The slate invokes `ResolveRecordedAlbumCampaign(..., programme: true)` for
   an LP advance opportunity **even without** `--album-release-practice`. The
   selective prior and incremental campaign test already apply to this route.
5. An ordinary slate single instead passes through `PrepareSlateMasterCore`,
   generates and pays for a new record, and reaches issue with `programmeMaster`.
   At issue, `programmeMaster ?? TryExtractSuccessfulAlbumSingle(...)` skips the
   catalogue alternative. The extraction helper also requires
   `AlbumReleasePractice.IsLive`. Enabling the flag alone would not make already
   prepared slate singles compare with older LP cuts.

Thus there is a concrete **preparation-time master-allocation gap**, beyond raw
writer supply or a missing discovery flag. `--lp-assembly` addresses the opposite
direction—reusing controlled singles/shelved masters on LPs—and can reduce new
session demand, but does not implement that missing singles comparison.

The next allocation intervention should offer a legitimate controlled LP master
at single preparation, compare its perceived suitability and incremental costs
with the ordinary session, consume the existing single slot, and preserve
ownership, release timing, campaign/pressing costs and the paid performance.
Prospective LP advances and post-release extraction require different timing
rules; the existing extraction helper requires an 8–52-week-old Top 40 LP and
its own economics/funding checks. Do not silently remove those guards or make
every album song a free single.

The current logs do not retain a contemporaneous perceived single-read or an
alternative project quote for each rejected LP cut, and do not contain the full
master-control history or contract sequence in this join. Composition hook is
not act fit, realized master quality, perceived quality, or profit. Weekly
snapshots cannot establish event order within a week; “unprepared” is a snapshot
classification, not proof that reassignment was possible at that exact instant.
The observed later release is not a counterfactual return estimate.

### Features that are already active despite absent enable tags

- Scene exports enable genre market, population lifecycle, evolution,
  recognition, managers, opening careers, local lifecycle/radio fatigue,
  hometown audiences, cowriting, team writing, world churn, wealth, fatigue,
  musician growth, member identity/axes and life exits.
- Polar selection defaults on in `SimulationSeedBootstrap`; the album material
  planner defaults on in `Data/AlbumMaterialPlan.cs`.
- Project judgement implies perceived repertoire selection and anticipatory
  material search. Both are also explicit in the experiment's runner.
- The ordinary profile supplies artist-fanbase recruitment/retention, hit-act
  support/cadence/retention, slate allocation/capacity/work budgets/breakout
  planning, studio labels, and the full LP retail envelope.
- `LabelCorporateModel.DefaultBundleRequested` enables corporate accounts
  without `--label-corporate-model`. Its implicit correction dependencies also
  enable maturity curves, career-banded memory, promo feedback, album career
  milestones, advance recoupment, royalty tiers, mechanical publishing,
  cross-collateralized renewal, LP launch reserve, period prices, the studio
  modifier correction and self-excluded artist heat. These should not be
  incorrectly reported missing just because their tags are absent.
- Contact networks, session employment/growth/record lift, repertoire influence,
  work migration, and the player studio rollout are default-enabled in their
  owning services. Local scenes have their own default/dependency resolution.

This audit identifies genuine pending integration gaps; it does **not** conclude
that the current profile represents the intended final game. The safe integration
deliverable is one explicit, versioned target profile with resolved dependencies,
followed by the branch-end decade evaluation. Preserve these matched results as
current-profile evidence and repeat the most useful comparisons on that target.
No claim is made here that every absent experimental parameter should take an
arbitrary enabled value; mutually exclusive ablations and historical control
switches still need their intended settings selected.
