# Writing-market runs with causal fanbase

2026-11-02. Three fresh 104-week runs replay the combined planned-assignment and
delivery-offer writing-market control with the causal media/fanbase bundle enabled.
The controls are the original `plan-pilot-control` worlds. They did not enable
this bundle or the ordinary-game profile. Reminders are off in both arms. A
fourth exploratory run adds the complete ordinary-game profile to seed 1001's
fanbase run. No default or game mechanics were changed.

The comparison uses assembly
`511E9709FE20879CE4636C7FFE39F9C62E9CE825DC8EB01A110041711D221AC5` in every
run. The matched treatment adds only `--causal-media-atmosphere`,
`--media-recognition`, `--fame-registration`, `--observe-following`,
`--media-residencies`, and `--follower-demand`. This is a **bundle** comparison;
it does not isolate follower purchases from recognition, media or residencies.
The full-profile run adds `--default-simulation-profile` to that bundle.

A title counts once at its first observed non-opening Top 40 entry. The cohort
is based on each world's first observed non-opening hit and requires 52 weeks
of follow-up; a different set of acts can enter each arm. Slot leaders are the
30 highest-ranked acts in each world, not a fixed set of identities. Silence
counts cohort acts without any subsequent single launch in 52 weeks. Cohort
percentages are descriptive world outcomes, not paired individual effects.

The bundle moved outcomes strongly: each seed has more hit titles and less
post-hit release silence, but fewer hit acts, a smaller 1–3-hit career middle,
and more concentration. It does not demonstrate that follower demand alone is
responsible. Full trajectories and cohorts change, and two years are insufficient
to establish a general balance verdict.

The full profile produces more hit acts and lower concentration in all three
worlds, and more acts in the complete 1–3-repeat cohort in absolute numbers.
The proportion in that cohort falls in two seeds and rises in one; the cohort
membership changes, so neither the count nor the percentage is an individual
effect. Post-hit release silence falls in two seeds and rises slightly in one.
The profile therefore supports a broader hit landscape, but does not establish
that audience memory alone causes it or that the repeat-career rate improves.

Professional material remains uncommon in the full profile. Across its three
worlds, 61,762 of 64,170 unique sampled professional entries at first mature
programme reviews failed the combined label-level license check; 17,401 of
19,587 reviewed plans had neither an accessible sampled professional song nor
an owned delivery. These are sampled entries and first reviews, not unique
songs or all routes to material. The check combines availability, rights,
holds and affordability. Full-profile access and career outcomes remain
descriptive: no access intervention was tested here, so these runs cannot tell
whether improving songwriter access would improve career breadth.

A subsequent three-seed, matched full-profile access experiment now tests that
question separately; see [WritingFullProfileAccessInvestigation.md](WritingFullProfileAccessInvestigation.md).
Accessible demo sampling raises the complete-window professional share from
5.76% to 11.32% pooled and moves mature issued plans from 10.28 to 5.01 covers
per professional song. Writing fees rise in every seed and concentration rises
in two, so the ratio gain does not establish a general career-balance improvement.

## Other consequential omitted settings

The old writing controls omitted `--default-simulation-profile`. Its current
contents in `Systems/SimulationArguments.cs` include:

- The distinct `--artist-fanbase` regional returning-buyer model, its .35
  recruitment and 104-week attachment half-life. This is separate from the media
  following/follower-demand model.
- Recent-hit commercial support (`--hit-act-hype=0.3` and
  `--hit-act-fit-floor=0.6`, with the profile's commercial-fit parameters).
- `--hit-act-retention` and `--hit-act-cadence`, which affect continued contracts
  and release opportunities, along with slate, work-budget and breakout planning.
- Stocking/reorders, economic and LP/retail settings, among many other changes.

The full default profile is not equivalent to adding only these named flags. The
later four-seed fresh programme comparisons already exercised the ordinary
profile alongside causal media/follower settings; see
`ProgrammeConcentrationInvestigation.md`. This test additionally runs the finite
writing market and combined writing workflow with that combination. It does not
independently establish how the two audience models interact.

exited successfully and emitted their complete 104-week markers without reported
engine errors. The comparison analyzers verify identical assembly and matched
flags, complete endpoints, material provenance, reconciled commission ledgers,
