## Implemented boundary

`--enable-scene-recruitment` gates the new economic readers. Persistence and rooms
retain their existing defaults; geographic recruitment is off by default.
Dependencies and contradictory observation/disable flags are validated. An
unrecognized dynamics flag still fails explicitly.

Initialization, native fresh/experienced discovery, recovery, player-crowding
rivals and final daily/weekly commitments share one geographic access policy.
Contract choice reads catchment/road/guest locality rather than region equality.
Recovery cannot widen a small label's supply to an unexplained national pool or
give it four times the ordinary attention budget. Existing birth, activation,
hold, ownership, affordability, contract and roster services retain authority.

Routes are same catchment, a reachable domestic road scouting radius, an existing
dated guest participation at HQ, or a capable domestic major's national A&R.
The `RoadCircuit` diagnostic denotes the provisional road scouting radius; it
does not assert an authored promoter/referral graph. Radius defaults are 225 miles
for Small/Boutique, 350 Independent, 650 MidTier and 900 Major. National A&R requires
Major tier plus native scouting and national reach of at least 0.5. These are
gameplay hypotheses, not historical measurements. Native distribution or borrowed
trucks do not provide an independent with national recruitment access.

Road and national weights favor nearby candidates. Runtime attention uses stable
weighted sampling without replacement in the existing four-week window, with the
native slate size. Queries consume neither shared RNG nor population RNG. The
initialization score still uses its existing seeded quality/risk evaluation and
ten-candidate selection, filtered and weighted by access. It is not a historical
record of years of prelaunch scouting.

Unknown literal HQs and satellite routes lacking a playable catchment remain
unresolved rather than using their distribution proxy or zero miles. Foreign
local access exists; cross-border recruitment requires actual guest presence.
No licensing/referral links, overseas road trips, new offices, automatic moves or
extra talent are invented. Dedicated referral/office connections and finer access
constraints remain later implementation work.

Each new AI signing retains IDs, the observed HQ/base/origin, date, route, road
distance when known, phase and guest evidence. Contracts retain working base and
hometown; they make no relocation promise. Save version 8 persists this evidence
inside the canonical artist. Legacy contracts remain unannotated; turning the
gate off does not remove stored history or recast existing rosters.
