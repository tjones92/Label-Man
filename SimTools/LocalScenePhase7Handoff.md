## Implemented behavior

They are source contexts, not new playable cities, US distribution regions, foreign consumer markets or newly simulated foreign labels.

A separate 3% keyed slice of compatible new acts uses the fictional specialist exchanges in Muscle Shoals, Bakersfield and Clovis. The source genre gates, national birth budget, quality, naming draws and sales-region fields are unchanged. Exact shares and relative source-city weights are authored gameplay priors, not historical population measurements. Explicit/inherited formation places win. Legacy acts are not assigned foreign origins just because their genre has a British name.

Capable existing majors active in the US market get a persistent finite portfolio of three named agency offices: London and two keyed regional connections. Existing UK-headquartered labels already selling into the US model can use their named source circuits. This creates no foreign sales economy. Other labels require an actual dated visitor or a paid introduction. An introduction confers A&R access, not artist travel, residency, master ownership, territorial licensing or distribution rights.

The A&R board offers a compact source-office selector. An introduction costs $150 and two hours, lasts 52 weeks and cannot be purchased twice while current. Reviewing available submissions costs two hours and shows at most four existing eligible acts. The batch and repertoire reads are keyed; repeated reviews do not create talent or reroll underlying quality. An empty office supplies no invented substitute and charges no review time. A full follow-up reads the submission rather than claiming a live performance abroad. Normal contract availability, ownership, affordability and negotiation rules remain authoritative.

New `ForeignAgency`, `SourceCircuit`, `SpecialistReferral` and `PlayerSourceDemo` evidence distinguishes business discovery from a live visitor or local bill. AI nominations recheck current access. Player follow-up and signing reject an expired/replaced introduction or rival ownership. Notebook entries retain the exact introduction ID through serialization and reload.

Pooled hiring, replacement, recombination and returning members respect known country identity. A US sales-region match cannot move a UK person into a US act, or vice versa. A newcomer generated by the existing person owner inherits its act's working base. Country safety remains for existing people even when new source creation is disabled. No international travel or immigration model is inferred from domestic road miles.

Save envelope v10 adds label connections and notebook demo provenance. AI labels serialize connections through the existing world contract; player labels capture detached connections explicitly. Assignment version 3 records the new procedural source-placement provenance. Existing identities, contracts, moves and sales projections remain intact. The source layer defaults on under persistent rooms and recruitment. `--disable-scene-extended-world` isolates it; explicit contradictory/dependency flags reject. Prior matched audit drivers explicitly exclude it so historical treatments retain their definitions.
