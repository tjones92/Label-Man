# Polar Gospel attribution

October 4, 2026. Attribution is complete for the retained starting cases and two bounded early-1961 worlds. The user asked to continue Gospel attribution while avoiding another hours-long census. The attached directive supplied research context; this task did not execute its later repair or acceptance packages. Musical tables, fit weights, access rules, suitability window and gameplay selection remain unchanged. Only development seeds 1001/1002 were used.

## Finding

The initial exclusion is primarily an act-identity mismatch against the differentiated Gospel forms. Identity accounts for 95.3% and 97.8% of the mean best-candidate score gap. The later seed divergence adds two mechanisms: large growth in accessible artist-authored compositions, and a market-taste term that favors competing material in seed 1001 while favoring Gospel material in seed 1002. Holding that term neutral makes the later inherited shares converge to 17.67% and 15.26%. This does not make either seed pass the provisional band.

The older single-archetype taxonomy makes the initial problem worse. Sacred-context attribution is unidentifiable: every inspected composition has unknown explicit subject/context. An outside songbook, origin label or musical archetype does not establish secular content. The evidence supports separate investigation of act identity, sparse market feedback and explicit context metadata; it does not support a genre quota or a global window change yet.

The Gospel genre prior is [0.5000, 0.3500, 0.9500, 0.7000] in Toughness, Sophistication, Sincerity and Maturity. The weighted center of its authored QuietHymn, GospelQuartet, GospelChoir and SpiritualShout forms is [0.3500, 0.3875, 0.9250, 0.7125]. Toughness has the largest raw discrepancy. The center diagnostic replaces that prior center, retains performer offsets and all other act attributes, and changes no stored actor. It is an attribution control, not an approved new prior. No single-axis experiment establishes that Toughness alone causes the effect.

The frozen-arrangement control is particularly useful: capability and realized music stay fixed, yet initial exclusion falls from 32/53 to 1/53 and 31/40 to 0/40. Re-resolving arrangements gives a similar initial result. The taxonomy control replaces Gospel-book reference archetype, pace and mood with the former SpiritualShout fallback; it leaves composition provenance and the selector’s ballad metadata fixed. Its worsening effect shows that restoring the old single form is not a supported repair. Reference-reading fit helps initially but tests a different musical interpretation, and is not a consistent later improvement.

The selector ranks suitability buckets of width 0.025, then preference and stable tie breaks; it does not discard every song outside the first bucket. Refusal follows ranking. “Outside top window” means no Gospel-book candidate can compete in that first bucket, not zero eligibility. Score-component removal leaves refusal fits and absolute bucket width fixed and does not renormalize weights, so those ablations include a score-scale effect. The center control avoids that problem.

## Context and subcohort coverage

All 112,978 candidate instances across the four worlds have unknown explicit subject/context. The probe checks composition contentTagIds and demo content tags for explicit sacred/religious/christian/worship and secular markers. It keeps mixed and unknown distinct. The strict sacred-only sensitivity has no known eligible covers and produces only the already allocated originals: this is missing metadata, not a measured failure of valid sacred repertoire. Restricting to the named Gospel book raises inherited share, but that proxy cannot identify sacred outside material or secular adaptations and is not a proposed policy.

The smallest metadata addition can use the existing persisted composition content fields with a defined unknown/sacred/secular/mixed vocabulary and explicit authorship evidence. Context should be assigned when material is authored or imported, retained through covers and saves, and reviewed independently of musical archetypes. Existing unknown compositions should remain unknown until supported; no retrofit by title, origin or genre. A separately staged context-fit experiment needs a defensible authored fixture before a population effect can be measured.

All inspected acts have known performers and a vocalist. The cohort function returns inheritedRepertoire for every Gospel act. It cannot distinguish quartet, choir or songwriter-led populations. Type and active-member counts are retained as structural proxies, not those semantic identities. A minimal extension would persist an explicit Gospel performance role (unknown/solo-ensemble/quartet/choir) and a separate repertoire orientation (unknown/inherited/songwriter-led); member counts or writing ability alone cannot establish both. These absent semantic cohorts are untested.

## Decision and validation

Do not tune the 50–90 band or introduce quotas. The isolated identity-center result is a useful candidate for a separately measured correction, but it still leaves seed 1001 at 38.02% later; re-resolving gives 37.91%. There is no accepted repair. The next experiments should stage explicit context metadata, Gospel identity consistency and the influence of sparse chart taste separately. A soft suitability selector remains a separate all-genre comparison; these results neither test it nor establish that it solves context or sparse-feedback errors. Cross-genre regressions, retention, set-fill acceptance and the hold-out remain for repair validation.

Build passes with zero errors and four existing warnings. Analysis validates CSV shape, actor/pool hashes, selected-slot uniqueness, provenance counts, weighted score-gap decomposition, table fingerprints, exact initial retained-probe agreement and unchanged worlds. Adding the neutral-moment control preserves both later candidate/actor files byte for byte and every existing selected variant exactly. The failed early fixture and superseded runs remain as diagnostic artifacts and are excluded from the evidence.
