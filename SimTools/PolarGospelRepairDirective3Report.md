# Gospel repair Directive 3 — implementation and evidence

October 5, 2026. Development seeds 1001 and 1002 only. No hold-out run.

Shape v2 is implemented, defaults on for new worlds, and passes deterministic, constraint, save/load, cover/scouting and cache-preservation checks. Its selector interaction is substantial: fresh Q3 1961 Gospel inherited shares are **23.42% and 33.36%**, compared with repaired v1 **43.21% and 46.56%**. The fixed-world later frames independently show large losses, so trajectory resume differences cannot explain the entire interaction. No selector weights, templates, bands, population rules or production admissions were retuned.

All four retained-world capture projections, actor/pool/original/request invariants and retained selection lines pass. V2 restores the preregistered nearest-neighbor diversity floor in every measured source/archetype group, and overall template overlap stays below the proposed 5% ceiling. Some groups fail the proposed readability criteria. Alice's visual review of [the 80-song sample sheet](C:/Project/Label-Man/SimTools/PolarShapeSampleSheet.html) remains required.

D2 increases accessible standards and improves later-frame inherited share, but it cannot establish a historical corpus. Research did not establish a common reachable canon larger than 350, numerical set-composition shares, or act-count ranges. **No Gospel songs were added.** `--gospel-songbook-v2=off` is a no-op; `on` explicitly rejects startup with exit 2. This is a reserved, tested gate, not a completed expansion feature. The requested expanded-book trajectories remain gated. The executed trajectories isolate shape v2 with the unchanged 350-song book.

The monthly A-coordinate replay does not reproduce the retained original Q1 Folk cohort totals. The original **18-slot quarterly movement remains causally unattributed**; local coordinate results are reported without substituting them for that missing causal confirmation.
