# Polar repertoire: research from the stopped runs

October 4, 2026. The user requested that the two final treatment processes stop and that existing data be researched. Processes 9932 and 14540, and their console wrappers 8216 and 12280, were stopped at 12:48 a.m. EDT. No new simulation, gameplay change, or hold-out consumption was performed. Raw files remain in place; no completion marker was fabricated.

The usable treatment window is **January 1960 through September 1961: 21 completed monthly censuses in each seed**. Both processes were inside October when stopped. October is excluded in its entirety, including syntactically complete rows, to prevent genre and population bias from a partially traversed census. This is an interrupted research report, not the directive’s four-year acceptance report.

## What the evidence is enough to decide

The repertoire repairs substantially break the old concentration collapse. Country, RnB, Folk, composer-led Jazz and vocal Surf lie inside their provisional bands in the observed window. Easy Listening and Jazz interpreters have small misses. Gospel has a large, replicated shortfall and is the next mechanism to investigate. Continuing the unchanged exhaustive census would improve temporal coverage, but it would not resolve that known shortfall.

Arrangement evaluation is unnecessary for assigning provenance categories. It affects suitability and therefore actual selection in the implemented model; replacing resolved fit with reference fit would test a different selector. The retained starting-world A/B already shows large ranking divergence. Future research can use a bounded sample of acts and fixed-world comparisons instead of recomputing every act’s entire candidate pool every month. The A/B measures ranking divergence, not proof that arrangement evaluation is responsible for a historical-share improvement.

## Scope and validation

Numerator: traditional lineage plus compositions established as standards in the observation year. Denominator: every filled live-set song slot, including newly authored material. Public-domain rights are separate from lineage. Earlier legacy percentages that combined standard-generation flags and public-domain material cannot be compared directly with this numerator.

Millions of repeated slots are not millions of independent observations: these are two simulated worlds, and the same acts recur.

Baseline and treatment trajectories can differ in act membership, signing, and chart outcomes. The comparisons below are equal-window descriptive comparisons, not fixed-population causal estimates. The separate six-stage January probe holds actor identities, genres and skills fixed. Cohort labels were not present in the baseline slot CSV, so baseline comparisons use genre totals.

The different trajectories do not support treating the aggregate shortfall as a uniform error to remedy with a quota. The code permits Gospel access to both Gospel and RhythmAndSoul source families. Music fit does not encode a sacred/secular suitability restriction. Whether the selected outside material is appropriate Gospel repertoire is an unresolved semantic question; adding religious-context metadata is a proposed remedy, not a measured treatment effect.

## Why exhaustive monthly auditing becomes expensive

| Seed / treatment | Acts censused in January 1960 | Acts censused in September 1961 | Candidate entries summed in January | Candidate entries summed in September |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1001 | 3000 | 10311 | 507,447 | 4,919,533 |
| 1002 | 3000 | 10266 | 489,487 | 4,722,341 |

Act counts grow from 3,000 to about 10,300; candidate entries summed across their pools grow roughly tenfold. These are measured census workloads, not profiler timings or exact resolver-call counts; rock uses a different selector. The code constructs a live proposal for each candidate in non-rock RankLive, and the resolver considers eligible arrangement rows. Act derivation, taxonomy derivation and candidate evaluation repeat many times. A sampled census controls audit cost; changing suitability semantics merely to make an audit faster would confound the experiment.

Repertoire admissions are buffered and written by FlushRepertoireAdmissions, currently called at normal close. Zero-byte interrupted admission files therefore mean unavailable evidence, not no admissions. Inventory is written only at normal close. Weekly audit output flushes every 52 weeks; treatment weekly rows stop at December 1960 even though the monthly census survives through September 1961. Recording and chart telemetry must not be assumed to cover the whole retained live window. The slot/set streams have a truncated October tail, which is excluded.

No 1962–63 treatment result, post-1962 contemporary-Folk calibration, Bossa calibration, dynamic long-term standard-promotion result, or hold-out 73019 confirmation is claimed. No saved monthly treatment worlds were generated by this audit, so the killed process state cannot be replayed from CSVs alone. Source hashes and invocation assembly/table hashes preserve provenance; hashes identify data, not completeness.

## Recommended next research, before another long audit

1. Use the retained Gospel fit cases to compare sacred-context eligibility, songbook taxonomy and act identity on identical pools. Attribute the shortfall before tuning a preference or access constant. Distinguish metadata correction from a musical-fit redesign.
2. Examine seed 1001’s growing cover selection from authored compositions and seed 1002’s stronger songbook access. Add composition subject/context to the diagnostic if that is the missing distinction.
3. Check Folk and Gospel retention regressions on fixed acts and fixed set sizes. Keep population diversity and within-act churn as separate acceptance measures.
4. Replace exhaustive monthly development censuses with deterministic samples stratified by genre, cohort, population and songwriting ability. Report denominators and absent cohorts. If creating later-year worlds without census, first verify on a short comparison that disabling observation does not change the simulated trajectory.
5. Flush research streams at census boundaries and add a graceful stop that closes writers and saves a world snapshot before any future long run. This is proposed infrastructure work; it has not been implemented in this task.
6. Revisit Easy Listening’s small misses and Jazz seed 1002 after the larger model issue is understood. Reserve the hold-out for confirmation after changes are settled.

The current evidence is sufficient to prioritize and design those bounded repairs. It is insufficient to sign off all phases of the original 1960–63 directive.

## Reproduction and artifacts

Run `node SimTools/analyze-polar-interrupted.mjs`, then `node SimTools/write-polar-interrupted-research.mjs`. Both read retained data; they do not start Godot. The analyzer writes a new research JSON and leaves the existing final-report/acceptance outputs untouched.

Machine-readable output: [polar-repertoire-interrupted-research.json](../SimLogs/polar-repertoire-interrupted-research.json). It includes monthly, quarterly, annual and aggregate partitions; cohort bands; family/origin/demo/arrangement distributions; concentration; pool counts; churn; first-observed population groups; fixed-world phases; fit probes; smoke admission/inventory evidence; excluded-row counts; unavailable streams; and SHA-256 hashes of all inputs and relevant sources.
