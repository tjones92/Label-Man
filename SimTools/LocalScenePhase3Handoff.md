## Implemented slice

All 31 playable cities have six persistent, explicitly fictional supporting institutions: a club, listening room, acoustic coffeehouse, community hall, roadhouse and trade listening appointment. IDs, booker IDs, names, programming context, provenance, capacities, admission and weekly rhythm are stable. Early and late programming descriptions retain continuing traditions. These are authored gameplay profiles drawn from the existing historical atlas, not claims that the fictional rooms existed or that their capacities, exact schedules or 1965 transition were historical facts. The taxonomy and common numerical defaults are provisional; richer room equipment, prestige, access barriers, seasons and contextual audience behavior remain authoring/calibration work.

A deterministic rolling two-week calendar contains canonical artist IDs, the actual active person IDs captured at booking, opening/closing roles, existing repertoire references, recurring engagements and cancellation state. World clock advancement resolves each set at its end, independently of player visits. Changed lineups/careers/presence cancel the original appearance without a fresh-act substitution. Shared musicians cannot overlap or use multiple rooms on the same day. This conservative reservation is stricter than a future within-city travel model. Residency and coarse dated presence reuse Phase 2 participation; the scene service creates no tours, acts, people, compositions or contracts.

Local draw uses room capacity and bounded past performances at that room, separately from national sales and hidden musical quality. Completed bills record attendance, response, modeled door receipts and a half-door fee allocation. Trade appointments grant no stage hours or performance fees. Fees are an accounting fact, not a new credit to label cash or a musician wealth model.

The existing A&R board shows named handbills, actual upcoming casts, recurring engagements and room contacts. Scouting observes the shared bill/set, spends two hours and charges admission once. Coffeehouse/community access is free and requires no alcohol purchase. Signed residents and latent contract prospects remain hearable; their authoritative contract availability remains separate. Player knowledge/notebook stores the actual room, bill, booker and encounter date. Follow-up checks the player's city and the next real bill, reads that bill's shared set and updates encounter provenance; it cannot claim a stale prior set was heard again. Booker identity and observer knowledge are present; a developed contact relationship/referral network is later work.

## Ownership, accounting and controls

The scene seed uses a separate keyed namespace and consumes neither global Godot RNG nor population RNG. Native annual work owners receive the scene ledger and attribute real stage hours within their existing synthetic allowances. Growth and fatigue totals remain unchanged; unbudgeted stage hours are explicit. This prevents extra growth/fatigue for the same night while preserving the accepted control. It is the first accounting bridge, not full behavioral calibration of real work replacing the background work proxy. Calibrate that bridge before treating changed workloads or performance income as accepted economics.

Rooms default on when persistence is on. `--disable-scene-rooms` retains the Phase 2 persistent discovery bridge. `--disable-local-scenes`/`--disable-persistent-scenes` retain off controls; `--observe-local-scenes` remains identity only. Contradictory flags fail before changing configuration. Turning rooms off preserves stored metadata; re-enabling after a gap cancels frozen past bookings without backpay. Opening a calendar cannot bypass this rule.

Save version 7 stores the bounded calendar, current engagements, sparse annual person/act work summaries and room/act performance history. Room schema and catalog version are validated before mutating the world. World and player restoration precede relinking. Legacy saves start with future bills, with no invented past gigs, fees or practice. Current/future bills are bounded; work/history grows by participating IDs and year and still needs decade cost measurement.
